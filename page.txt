ABOUT US**************

   Welcome to Taiwan Center for Mandarin Learning!

   For years, Tzu Chi Academy has been deeply rooted in the Greater Vancouver
   community, promoting not only language education but also humanistic
   values and community service. In 2026, we are thrilled to expand our
   educational mission by establishing the "Taiwan Center for Mandarin
   Learning (TCML)" specifically designed for adult learners, in proud
   collaboration with Taiwan's Overseas Community Affairs Council (OCAC).

   At Tzu Chi, language learning is more than just memorizing vocabulary; it
   is a warm and engaging cultural exchange. We provide a professional,
   inclusive, and joyful environment where adults from all backgrounds can
   learn Mandarin while immersing themselves in the rich tapestry of
   Taiwanese culture--including its history, arts, and everyday life.

   Supported by the OCAC's global initiative, our tailored curriculum is
   designed to meet your unique needs, whether you are learning for career
   advancement, daily communication, or a passion for cultural exploration.
   We hope our center serves as a profound cultural bridge between Taiwan and
   Greater Vancouver, inviting more friends to discover the beauty of Taiwan,
   make meaningful connections, and love Mandarin! *******
